Field Kit — Aviary

Rank a call against bundled sketches, and keep a lifelist. Offline, with no microphone.

about

This is not a bird identifier. There is no trained acoustic model and no microphone here. The bundled calls are synthetic spectral sketches, not recordings, and the ranking is nearest-neighbour over them. The ranking, the confidence threshold, the “not a bird” band and the lifelist are real; the identification is not.

Rank a sketch

Pick a bundled call to see it rank first, or a speech-like sketch to see it refused. Confidence is scaled to the spread of these sketches, so a call unlike all of them scores low rather than least-bad.

    Log it

    A confirmed ID goes into the lifelist with its time, place and clip.
