Field Kit — Aviary

Rank a call against bundled sketches, and keep a lifelist with the time, the place and the clip. Offline, with no microphone.

Open Aviary

This is not a bird identifier. There is no trained acoustic model and no audio capture in this estate. The eight bundled calls are synthetic spectral sketches, not recordings, and the ranking is nearest-neighbour over them. What is real: the ranking, the confidence, the threshold, the “not a bird” band and the lifelist.

Status Built and working offline: eight bundled sketches; a ranked list with confidences; a threshold below which the app says not a bird and offers no species; a lifelist with time, place and clip that survives a reload; and a dawn-chorus tally that confirms nothing.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: recording, spectrogram computation from audio, and a trained classifier. None of those exist here, and the app says so above the fold rather than shipping a microphone button that would return confident nonsense.

What it does

  • Ranks a spectral sketch against eight bundled templates and shows a confidence for each.
  • Refuses to name a species below the threshold: it says not a bird instead.
  • Keeps a lifelist — species, time, place, clip — that survives a reload with no network.
  • Tallies species over a session in dawn-chorus mode, without confirming any of them.

What it will not do

  • Identify a real bird. There is no model, and the app says so on its own face.
  • Record audio. No microphone, no capture, no spectrogram from sound.
  • Give a confident wrong ID. Below the threshold, no species is offered.
  • Confirm from the chorus. The tally is a tally.
  • Use the internet. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA sketch with the wrong number of bands is refused; a sighting with no place is refused; a species that is not bundled cannot be confirmed.
No networkNothing changes; the lifelist is on the device.
Storage full or unavailableNOT SAVED, and the sighting is not in the lifelist.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with an empty lifelist.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js.
